# Prompts das imagens de modalidades

Ativos gerados em 23 de agosto de 2026 com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, no modo padrão da habilidade de geração de imagens. A captura de cards verdes foi usada apenas como referência de hierarquia e fotografia de produto; nenhuma imagem, embalagem, marca, texto ou paleta da referência foi copiada.

## Base compartilhada

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: transparent product-style cutout for a Brazilian consortium category card
Style/medium: premium photorealistic 3D product photography, refined commercial catalog render, crisp realistic materials
Composition/framing: one single subject only, centered, front three-quarter view, fully visible, generous transparent padding, square canvas, consistent scale for a five-item card collection
Lighting/mood: soft studio key light, controlled highlights, subtle grounded contact shadow, calm and aspirational
Color palette: off-white and warm ivory as primary surfaces, deep navy blue details, one very restrained red accent
Constraints: genuinely transparent background with preserved alpha; no floor or backdrop; no people; no text; no letters; no numbers; no logos; no trademarks; no watermark; no frame; no UI; no extra decorative props; realistic clean silhouette
```

## Assuntos finais

- `imoveis.webp`: “a sophisticated miniature model of a contemporary Brazilian two-story home, white architectural volumes with warm wood detail and one small navy element”.
- `veiculos.webp`: “a modern premium compact SUV, warm pearl-white paint, dark navy glass and trim, tiny restrained red detail; front three-quarter angle, all wheels visible, no license plate text”.
- `motos.webp`: “a modern premium street motorcycle, deep navy bodywork with ivory panels and one tiny restrained red detail; front three-quarter angle, both wheels fully visible, no rider”.
- `servicos.webp`: “a refined professional service toolbox, ivory hard-shell case standing open just enough to reveal one polished navy-handled wrench; organized and premium, no scattered tools”.
- `planejamento-patrimonial.webp`: “one cohesive premium tabletop sculpture representing long-term patrimonial growth: an elegant warm-ivory architectural column integrated with three ascending deep-navy cylindrical steps and one restrained matte-red upward arc; centered and fully visible; genuinely transparent background; no text, currency signs, logos, watermark, frame or UI”.

## Arquivos publicados
